# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Discord server operators and community staff who create, share, and review archived channel transcripts. Carl also uses the private admin and health dashboards to operate the service. They need the transcript to read like the original channel so conversations can be understood quickly and confidently.

## Product Purpose

Generate durable, self-contained Discord channel transcripts that preserve message identity, chronology, content, and familiar Discord presentation. Success means an exported transcript feels like viewing the source channel rather than reading a generic log.

## Brand Personality

Familiar, faithful, unobtrusive. The interface should disappear behind the conversation and feel comfortable during extended review.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic activity feed, centered system-event log, stark document page, or decorative chat mockup. Avoid layouts that hide authorship, collapse ordinary messages into events, or diverge from Discord's established channel conventions.

## Design Principles

- Preserve conversation meaning before adding presentation.
- Reuse Discord's familiar hierarchy and interaction vocabulary.
- Keep long transcript-reading sessions calm and comfortable.
- Treat unusual message types as exceptions, never as the default.
- Make the static export useful without depending on the original Discord client.
- Protect private health and administration surfaces with standalone sign-in methods (passkeys and/or a dashboard password) owned by the service itself. Never fall back to a shared dashboard secret or an external identity provider.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain practical readability, visible keyboard focus, semantic markup, and reduced-motion behavior where applicable. Exact Discord fidelity and comfortable daily use are the primary goals; no formal conformance target is required.
